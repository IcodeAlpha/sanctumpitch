The Problem
1 in 4 Africans will experience a mental health condition. Yet Africa has just 1.4 psychiatrists per 100,000 people — and 90% of those who need care receive none. Existing apps are built for Western markets: expensive, culturally irrelevant, and inaccessible.
The Solution

An always-available AI wellness companion.

  • Conversational AI — empathetic, private, 24/7 emotional support
  • Private Journal — encrypted, safe space for thoughts and feelings
  • Smart Insights — AI-powered emotional pattern recognition
  • Wellness Activities — breathing, reflection, personalised exercises
  • Mobile App in active development (iOS + Android)
Business Model

Freemium → Subscription → B2B

  • Free tier drives organic user acquisition and retention
  • Subscription: $1.99/mo or $25.99/yr — unlocks AI companion + Insights
  • B2B (Year 2): corporate wellness, university licenses, NGO contracts
  • Target gross margin: ~70% (pure digital SaaS)
  • Break-even target: Month 18 with 400+ paying subscribers
